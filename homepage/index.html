Specifications

Languages

English, Deutsch, Español (LatAm), Français, Português (Brasil), 中文 （简体）, 日本語, and more ‐ translated by the community and open to additions.

Minimum Android

Android 12 or newer (API 31). Alarms are scheduled with setAlarmClock(), so no exact-alarm opt-out is needed on modern Android.

Permissions

  • USE_EXACT_ALARM ‐ fire alarms at the minute you set
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS ‐ show alarm and warning notifications
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE ‐ keep the reliability guard alive (optional layer)
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED ‐ re-arm alarms after reboot
  • WAKE_LOCK, VIBRATE ‐ wake and vibrate at alarm time
  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE ‐ holiday calendar downloads only

No location permission. Ever.

Privacy, verifiable

The complete evidence ‐ exact permission list, the one outbound holiday-network call and what it sends, the store Data safety disclosure ‐ is documented on the privacy page. Alarms, schedules and settings never leave your device.

Getting Started

Welcome to Workday Wake! Here's how to get started:

  1. Workday Wake is not yet released. The app is in preparation for its first release; this page will link the install options as soon as a build is published.
  2. Open the app and grant notification access
  3. Select your country and region so the app can load your local public holidays
  4. Configure your workdays in the settings menu
  5. Create your first alarm by tapping the + button

Configuration Guide

Work Schedule

By default, Workday Wake assumes a Monday-Friday work week. To customize:

  1. Go to Settings > Work Schedule
  2. Tap on the days you work
  3. Select your typical work hours

Alarm Settings

Each alarm can be configured with:

  • Time: When the alarm should ring
  • Label: A name for the alarm (e.g., "Morning Commute")
  • Sound: Choose from available alarm sounds
  • Vibration: Enable or disable vibration
  • Snooze: Allow snoozing and set duration
  • Active Days: Override the global work schedule for this specific alarm

Region Settings

Workday Wake picks public holidays for the country and region you choose. It uses that to:

  • Load the official public holidays for that country and subdivision
  • Provide region-specific workday defaults

There is no automatic location detection. The app does not request any location permission and never reads your GPS ‐ your timezone comes from your device's system settings, not from a location fix.

Choosing your region:

  1. Go to Settings > Change location
  2. Search for your country and select it
  3. If your country has states or regions, select yours
  4. The app refreshes the holiday calendar for that region

You can also set this during onboarding, or from the dashboard's “Configure location” prompt. Until a region is selected, holiday skipping stays inactive and alarms ring on every day.

Custom Holidays

In addition to automatic holiday detection, you can add your own special days:

Adding a Single Holiday

  1. Go to Settings > Holidays
  2. Tap Add Holiday
  3. Select the date and give it a name (e.g., "Summer Vacation")
  4. Save the holiday

Adding Recurring Holidays

For holidays that repeat every year (like your birthday or anniversary):

  1. Go to Settings > Holidays
  2. Tap Add Recurring Holiday
  3. Select the month and day
  4. Give it a name and save

Troubleshooting

Alarms Not Ringing

Check the following:

  1. Ensure the alarm is enabled (toggle switch should be on)
  2. Verify the alarm time is set correctly
  3. Check that today is a workday in your schedule
  4. Make sure your phone's volume is up and Do Not Disturb is off
  5. Verify that the app has notification permissions

Alarms Ringing on Holidays

Possible causes:

  • No region is selected yet ‐ go to Settings > Change location
  • The holiday data for your region has not loaded (needs internet once)
  • You might have overridden the holiday in your custom settings

Solution: Add the holiday manually in Settings > Holidays

Battery Optimization Issues

Some Android devices aggressively optimize battery usage, which can prevent alarms from ringing. To fix this:

  1. Go to your phone's Settings > Battery > Battery Optimization
  2. Find Workday Wake in the list
  3. Select Don't optimize

Note: If you are using a device from Xiaomi, Realme, Oppo, Vivo, or Samsung, standard Android settings are not enough.
Please open the Workday-Wake app, navigate to Settings, and follow the specific 'Device Battery Guide' provided for your phone.

For the full picture of how Workday Wake survives these devices ‐ watchdog process, self-healing, diagnostics ‐ read the Alarm Reliability guide.

Widgets Not Updating

If your home screen widget is stuck showing "Morning alarm" or doesn't update immediately after you tap Skip:

  1. Open your phone's Recent Apps screen.
  2. Tap the three dots (or swipe down) on the Workday-Wake card.
  3. Select Lock. A lock icon will appear, and your widgets will now update instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Workday Wake available for iOS?

Currently, Workday Wake is exclusively available for Android. We do not have an iOS version planned at this time.

Does the app work offline?

Yes! Once you've chosen your region and work schedule, the app works completely offline. Holiday data is cached locally.

How does the app know about holidays?

Workday Wake uses publicly available holiday data for each country. When you select your region, the app downloads the relevant holiday calendar for it.

Can I sync alarms between devices?

No. Workday Wake has no accounts and no cloud sync, and your alarms are never uploaded anywhere. Setup happens per device: install the app on each device and choose your work schedule and region there.

Is my data private?

All your alarm data, chosen region, and custom settings are stored locally on your device. See the privacy page for the verifiable evidence: exact permission list, the one outbound network call, and the Data safety link.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Please open an issue on our GitHub repository with as much detail as possible, such as your device model, Android version, and steps to reproduce the issue.
