Support Workday Wake

Workday Wake is free, carries no advertising, and has no accounts or analytics.

There is nothing to buy and nothing is behind a paywall. If it is useful to you, here are the ways to help ‐ and a few that cost nothing at all.

Ways to say thanks

  • Play version: a small tip inside the app (Play tip jar). Tips unlock nothing — the app is complete without them.
  • Direct APK version: a one-off coffee or monthly support via Ko-fi, or, if you prefer a Chinese payment option, there is a donation link for that too. Both open in your browser; the app receives nothing back.

Free ways that help just as much

  • Report a bug you actually hit. A clear reproduction is worth more than a vague complaint, and it is the single most useful thing you can send.
  • Translate the app. The app ships with English, German and Simplified Chinese strings. Translation happens outside the code — write to the developer via the issue tracker and you will get the strings to fill in.
  • Star the repository and tell a colleague who keeps waking up on public holidays.

Where the money goes

Development time, the occasional device to test OEM battery behaviour on, and the store fees that distribution costs. There is no marketing budget, no analytics service to pay for, and no third party in the middle.

Privacy

The app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and your alarms never leave your device. See the privacy policy.

Other questions

Bugs, feature requests and anything else go to the public issue tracker.
