Privacy Policy

Workday Wake — last updated 4 October 2026

The short version. Workday Wake has no accounts, no servers of its own, no advertising and no analytics. Your alarms and settings stay in your device's own storage. The app contacts the network only to download public holiday calendars and, if you use the optional tip jar, to reach the payment provider your store uses.

What we collect

Nothing is collected. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend that we operate. We cannot see your alarms, your labels, your working hours, or your custom holidays, because none of it ever leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored in the app's private, on-device storage (a local database and a local preferences file). None of it is uploaded, backed up to us, or readable by us or anyone else:

  • Your alarms — time, label, repeat pattern, ringtone, volume, vibration and snooze settings
  • Your working week and per-alarm day patterns
  • Your custom holidays, including any calendar files you import
  • Your country/region selection, and UI preferences such as theme and language

What leaves your device, and why

The app makes outbound requests only to the hosts below, always over HTTPS. Each request is a lookup for public holiday data for the country/region you selected. It does not contain your alarms, your settings, a device identifier, or an advertising identifier.

DestinationWhyWhat is sent
openholidaysapi.org Public and school holiday calendars Country code, region code and year
date.nager.at Fallback holiday source if the primary one does not answer Country code and year
feiertage-api.de Fallback holiday source for German-speaking regions State code and year
peterandree.github.io Fetches the optional list of project supporters Ordinary web request; nothing identifying
Google Play Billing (Play version only) Optional tip jar Handled entirely by Google Play; the app receives a purchase token, never payment details
Ko-fi or Afdian (direct version only) Optional tip jar, where Play Billing is absent Nothing but an ordinary link tap; the payment page opens in your browser and the app receives nothing back

Holiday calendars are cached on your device so the app keeps working offline. You can clear the cache at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings.

Permissions, and what each one is for

The app requests only the permissions it needs to ring an alarm. It requests no location permission — your country and region are chosen by you from a list, and the app never reads your GPS position.

PermissionPurpose
USE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENTRing your alarm at the exact minute and show it over the lock screen
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShow alarm and reminder notifications
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDRe-arm your alarms after a reboot
FOREGROUND_SERVICE (+ system-exempted / special-use)Keep the alarm reliable on manufacturers that aggressively kill background apps
WAKE_LOCK, VIBRATEWake the device and vibrate when an alarm fires
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEDownload holiday calendars

Analytics, advertising and tracking

There is none. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting service, and no fingerprinting. It embeds no third-party tracking code and sets no cookies.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because your data never reaches us, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. You can erase everything the app holds by uninstalling it, or selectively by clearing its storage in Android Settings.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app transmits, this page will be updated before that version is distributed, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the project's public issue tracker.
